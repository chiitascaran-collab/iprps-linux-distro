Where your game lives

The biggest difference between IPRPS OS and online tabletop websites.

Online tabletop websites

Roll20, Owlbear Rodeo, Alchemy, D&D Beyond and others

  • Everyone needs the internet the whole time
  • Accounts and logins, often a subscription
  • Made for many game systems, or for one (D&D)
  • Great for battle maps, tokens and play over long distances

IPRPS OS

A whole computer system made for the Session Master

  • Runs games with no internet at all
  • No accounts for players, no subscription
  • Built for IPRPS: its rolls, bands and five combat modes
  • Private AI helper on your own computer (Medium and up)

Reading an IPRPS roll: the two bands

The Table, the Dice Roller and the Combat Tracker name the band for you. This is what it means.

Band A
Yes or no?
Failure1 – 6
Zone of Uncertainty7 – 12 · the SM decides
Success13 – 20
Band B
How well?
Weak1 – 6
Standard7 – 14
Strong15 – 19
Peak20

The band comes from the d20 alone. The total (d20 + Skill Shift + Attribute Shift) is compared with the BTN. Example: Brenna rolls a 9, plus 3 for Tracking 30 and plus 4 for WIS 14, for a total of 16. That beats BTN 14, and the 9 lands in the Zone of Uncertainty, so she succeeds, but with a twist the SM chooses.
