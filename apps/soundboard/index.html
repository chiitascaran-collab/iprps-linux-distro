Your sounds

    Your own recordings and music. Drop audio files anywhere to add them, then + Pad puts one on the board. The built-in sounds are made by the computer and need no files.

    With the IPRPS Table open, a pad set to Also on players' devices plays your file on the players' phones and laptops too (one sound at a time).
